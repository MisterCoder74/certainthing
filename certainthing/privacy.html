5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Storage Location

All user data is stored on our EU-based server infrastructure using a JSON-based persistence layer. Data is organized in segregated individual user folders identified by User ID.

5.2 Data Structure

Your personal data folder contains:

  • config.json — Your API key and settings
  • sessions/ — Project sessions and generated code
  • history.json — AI interaction history

5.3 Security Measures

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Server-side session management with secure cookies
  • File permissions restricting unauthorized access
  • Regular security updates and monitoring

6. Cookies and Session Data

6.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit a website. They serve various purposes, from remembering your login session to tracking browsing behavior across sites.

6.2 Cookies We Use

CertainThing uses only one cookie, which is strictly necessary for the Service to function:

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
PHPSESSID Strictly necessary (technical) Maintains your authenticated session while you use the Service. Without this cookie, the Service cannot recognize you as a logged-in user. Expires when you close your browser, or after the server-side session timeout

Legal Basis: Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) and exemption under Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive — strictly necessary cookies do not require user consent.

6.3 Cookies We Do NOT Use

  • No analytics or performance cookies (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • No advertising or marketing cookies
  • No third-party tracking cookies
  • No social media cookies
  • No profiling or behavioral targeting cookies

6.4 Managing Cookies

You can configure your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. However, if you disable cookies, you will not be able to use the Service as authentication requires the session cookie to function.

9. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject in the EU, you have the following rights:

9.1 Right of Access (Art. 15)

You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

9.2 Right to Rectification (Art. 16)

You can update your profile information within the app settings.

9.3 Right to Erasure (Art. 17)

You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting info@vivacitydesign.net.

9.4 Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)

You can export all your project data using the one-click download (ZIP) feature within the app.

9.5 Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18)

You can request temporary restriction of data processing.

9.6 Right to Object (Art. 21)

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.

9.7 Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3))

You can withdraw consent at any time by logging out and requesting account deletion.

10. Data Breach Notification
